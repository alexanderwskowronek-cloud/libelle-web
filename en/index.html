Frequently asked questions

The reading is not accurate. What can I do?

Every motion sensor has a small offset. Under Settings → Calibrate, Libelle measures it in two steps and removes it from then on. The surface does not even need to be level. A curved case or a camera bump can distort the result.

What is included in Libelle Pro?

Libelle Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription and works for the whole family through Family Sharing. It adds the Watch display, motorhome and caravan levelling, and the corner angle. Level, angle, picture and slope stay free.

I bought Pro but it is still locked.

Open Tools, tap a Pro tool and then “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

The Watch shows nothing.

The Watch does not measure by itself, it shows the value from the iPhone. Open Libelle on the iPhone and the Watch app on your watch. Both must be paired and Bluetooth must be on.

Does the camera in Picture record anything?

No. Libelle only shows the live image with guide lines. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
