Privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · applies to the app Libelle – Spirit Level for iPhone and Apple Watch

In short: Libelle collects no personal data. There is no account, no server of our own, no ads and no tracking.

The developer of the app Libelle

2. What the app processes

Libelle sends no data to us or to third parties. The following is processed on your device only:

  • Motion sensors: tilt and angles are calculated from the iPhone's motion sensors and are not stored.
  • Camera: only in Picture and only if you allow access. Libelle shows the live image with guide lines. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Settings and calibration, such as sound, slope target or your vehicle's dimensions, are kept in local app storage.
  • Apple Watch: with Libelle Pro, the iPhone sends the reading directly to your paired watch over the connection between your own devices, not through a server.

There is no analytics, no third-party crash reporting and no advertising or tracking technology. No third-party SDKs are included.

3. Purchases

You buy Libelle Pro through the App Store. Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Apple, we receive no payment or account data. The app checks on your device via Apple's StoreKit whether Pro is unlocked. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.

4. Ratings

Libelle rarely asks for a rating through Apple's dialog. We receive no data from you in the process.

5. Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You remove local data by deleting the app. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

6. Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes data differently, we will update this policy first.
